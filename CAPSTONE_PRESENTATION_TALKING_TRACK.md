#### THE 5-FACTOR ALGORITHM (Detailed Breakdown - 2.5 minutes)

**FACTOR 1: INTEREST OVERLAP (40% weight)**

[Point to the interest tags on card: Food, Culture]
Sarah selected during registration: Food tours, Cultural attractions, Photography
The browsing user selected: Food, Culture, Hiking, Photography

Match: Food ✓, Culture ✓, Photography ✓ = 3 out of 4 interests align.
Interest compatibility score: 75%

Why it matters: Shared interests mean shared trip activities. They'll actually want to do the same things.

---

**FACTOR 2: DATE ALIGNMENT (30% weight)**

Sarah's dates: February 10-20, 2026
Browsing user's dates: February 10-25, 2026

Overlap period: February 10-20 = 11 days of shared travel
The user stays until Feb 25, but can spend 11 days with Sarah.

Date compatibility: 90%

Why it matters: If dates don't align, the match is useless. Both users need to be traveling at the same time.

---

**FACTOR 3: BUDGET COMPATIBILITY (15% weight)**

Sarah's budget: $50-100 per day
Browsing user's budget: $60-120 per day

Shared budget window: $60-100 per day

Budget compatibility: 80%

Why it matters: If one person wants $20/day hostels and the other wants $200/night hotels, they're incompatible. Budget alignment is critical.

---

**FACTOR 4: DISTANCE/GEOFENCING (10% weight)**

Distance: 45 km away

Our geofencing tech finds people in the same geographic region who are planning trips at the same time. Nearby = practical for coordination.

Distance score: 85%

Why it matters: You can meet before the trip, split ride costs, coordinate logistics.

---

**FACTOR 5: COMMUNITY TRUST SCORE (5% weight)**

[Point to rating stars]
Sarah's profile shows: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.8 stars
Trips completed: 24
Verified: Yes (blue badge)

Trust score: 95%

Why it matters: If Sarah were a new user (no ratings) or had a 1-star rating, the algorithm would lower the compatibility score. Community verification adds confidence.

---

#### CALCULATING THE FINAL SCORE (30 seconds)

"Combine all five:

```
Compatibility = 
  (Interest Overlap 75% × 40%) +
  (Date Alignment 90% × 30%) +
  (Budget Match 80% × 15%) +
  (Distance 85% × 10%) +
  (Trust Score 95% × 5%)

= (30% + 27% + 12% + 8.5% + 4.75% + ...)
= 92%
```

The algorithm says: **'This person aligns with Sarah across multiple dimensions. 92% is a strong match.'**

This is data-driven matching, not guessing."

## 💬 COMMON QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

**Q: "What stops someone from lying about their interests?"**

A: "Good question. We have safeguards:
- ID verification (Step 2 prevents fake identities)
- Rating system: If someone lies and travelers call it out, they get low ratings
- Moderation team reviews flagged accounts
- Users can report/block matches"

---

**Q: "Does the algorithm get smarter over time?"**

A: "Yes. In production, we'd track:
- Who users actually match with
- Who they message
- Trip success rates
- User feedback

This trains our algorithm to predict better matches. But for this capstone, we're showing the deterministic version—the five clear factors. The foundation is solid."

---

**Q: "What about privacy? I don't want everyone seeing my interests."**

A: "Your interests are only shown to potential matches the algorithm generates. And only if they also match your date/location criteria. It's not like a public profile where strangers can browse. It's private data used for matching."

---

**Q: "Can I change my interests after signup?"**

A: "Yes. Users would have a profile settings page where they can edit interests anytime. Our algorithm recalculates matches whenever someone updates their profile."

---

**Q: "What if I'm traveling solo but don't want to match with others—just want to rent a vehicle?"**

A: "The same interests and budget data also inform carpool matching. You could rent from someone with similar interests (high rating), or just pick the cheapest option. Flexibility built-in."

## 🎓 Pro Delivery Tips

1. **Slow down on the algorithm.**
   - The 92% score is your strongest differentiator
   - Take time explaining the 5 factors
   - Use pauses: "Interest overlap... 75%. Date alignment... 90%."
   - Let it sink in

2. **Use your pointer/cursor.**
   - Direct attention to specific UI elements
   - Don't wave hands randomly
   - Let people read cards for 3-5 seconds before you talk

3. **Vary your tone.**
   - Excited when explaining matching
   - Thoughtful when breaking down algorithm
   - Confident when showing data

4. **Have numbers memorized:**
   - 92% (primary compatibility example)
   - 5 factors (interests, dates, budget, distance, trust)
   - 12 interests (why that number vs more/fewer)
   - 75%, 90%, 80%, 85%, 95% (the factor scores in your example)

5. **Anticipate questions:**
   - FAQ section above has 5 common ones
   - Prepare brief, clear answers
   - If you don't know something, say "That's a great question—I'd need to check that" rather than making something up
